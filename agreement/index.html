Website Build Agreement

This agreement is between ServiceFlow AI LLC, an Alabama limited liability company ("we" or "us"), and the client named below ("you"). It covers one website build. It is written in plain language on purpose. If any part of it is unclear, call us at (251) 270-2313 before you sign.

Your business

1 · What we build

A mobile first website for your business, built to be found in local search and to turn visitors into phone calls. Included:

  • A professionally designed site on your own domain, built to load fast and read well on a phone.
  • Local search setup on every page: unique page titles and descriptions, LocalBusiness structured data, and a submitted sitemap.
  • A working lead form (name, phone, email, service needed, message) delivered straight to your inbox, with a written consent line recorded on every submission.
  • Click to call buttons and basic visitor tracking.
  • Your Google Business Profile created or optimized and linked to the site. Google requires the business owner to complete its own ownership verification step. We prepare everything and walk you through it, but only you can finish it.

2 · Price and payment — select one

The build fee is due in full before work begins. We will send a secure payment link after you submit this form. The charge will appear on your card or bank statement as SERVICEFLOW AI.

Your domain registration is paid by you and registered in your name, typically $12 to $20 per year. We run the availability search and walk you through the purchase so the domain is yours from day one.

3 · Our two guarantees

Live in 14 days from kickoff, or the build is free.

Kickoff is the day both of these have happened: the build fee is paid, and we have received your materials (photos, logo, business details, and domain access). From that day we have 14 calendar days to put your site live. If we miss it, the build fee is refunded in full and you keep the site. If we are waiting on something from you, the clock pauses for those days and restarts when we get it.

30 day money back, no questions asked.

For 30 days after your site goes live you may ask for a full refund of the build fee. Your domain is yours and stays yours either way. The site we built comes down.

4 · What you own

  • The domain is registered in your name from day one. We never hold it.
  • The website and its files are yours once the build fee is paid and retained. If you take the 30 day refund, the fee is returned and the site and its files come down. Your domain is yours either way. If we miss the 14 day guarantee, you keep both the refund and the site.
  • Every lead is yours and yours alone. We do not resell, share, or route your leads to anyone else, including your competitors.
  • No contracts. Nothing here locks you into an ongoing plan.

5 · What we do not promise

We do not promise search rankings, lead volume, or revenue. Google decides rankings on its own timeline and no one can guarantee a position. Anyone who tells you otherwise is not being straight with you. What we do promise is in Sections 1 and 3: the site itself, the timeline, the ownership, and the two guarantees.

6 · What we need from you

  • Your business details, service list, service area, and hours.
  • Photos of your work, and your logo if you have one. If you do not, we will discuss options.
  • Access to your domain, or agreement to purchase one.
  • Review and approval at the draft stage. Please respond within three business days. Days spent waiting on you pause the 14 day clock.

7 · Photos, reviews, and content

Any stock or computer generated imagery used in a preview or draft is a labeled placeholder and is swapped for your real photos before launch. We never present an image as a photo of work you did when it is not. Testimonials and reviews on your site are real and come from your own public reviews or from customers you provide. We do not write fake reviews.

Two rounds of revisions are included during the build. Changes requested after launch are covered by a Care Plan if you have one, or quoted separately if you do not. New pages, new services, or a second location are outside this build and quoted separately.

Monthly plans start after launch and may be cancelled at any time with no penalty.
