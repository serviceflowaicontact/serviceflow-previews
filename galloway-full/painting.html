Home / Painting

Paint jobs are prep jobs. We do the prep.

Anyone can roll color on a wall. The difference you pay a pro for is everything that happens first — and how clean the lines are when the tape comes off.

Around here, paint is armor. Humidity, salt air, and sun break down a lazy exterior job in two summers. We scrape, sand, prime, caulk and seal before a brush ever touches finish paint — inside, we protect your floors and furniture like it's our own house.

One crew, one standard, interior and exterior. And because we're carpenters too, we fix the rotten trim and soft sheetrock other painters just paint over.

Painting we take on

  • Full exterior repaints — siding, trim, doors, shutters
  • Interior rooms and whole homes — walls, ceilings, trim
  • Rot and sheetrock repair before painting, not around it
  • Cabinets and built-ins
  • Pressure washing and surface prep
