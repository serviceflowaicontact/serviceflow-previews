Who you're hiring

David Galloway, and a standard that doesn't move.

Galloway Construction and Repair LLC is a family-run outfit based in Silverhill, in the middle of Baldwin County. Owner David Galloway brings more than thirty years in the trades, and the company has carried an A+ rating from the Better Business Bureau since becoming accredited in 2023, alongside 5.0 stars on HomeAdvisor.

The reviews all say versions of the same three things: they show up when they said, the work is high quality, and the price is the price that was quoted. That's not a slogan — it's just how you stay in business in a county where everybody knows everybody.

We're licensed with the Alabama Home Builders Licensure Board (#28159), we quote in writing, and we treat a small repair with the same respect as a full build — because the small job is how you earn the big one.
