Home / Decks & Patios

Decks & patios built for Gulf Coast weather.

Salt air, summer storms, and hard sun eat cheap deck work alive. We frame to code, flash it right, and fasten with hardware that lasts — so it still feels solid in ten years.

A deck here isn't decoration — it's where you live half the year. We build new decks, rebuild the ones that scare you, and repair the ones that just need honest work: new boards, new rails, re-set posts, fresh stain.

Screen enclosures too — one of our best-reviewed jobs was a sunroom and patio enclosure repair. If it's attached to the back of your house, it's our kind of work.

What we build and fix

  • New decks and rebuilds — framed to code, permitted where required
  • Deck repair — boards, rails, stairs, footings, re-leveling
  • Screen rooms and patio enclosures — new and repaired
  • Pergolas and covered patios
  • Cleaning, staining and sealing to survive the salt air
