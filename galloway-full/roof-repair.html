Home / Roof Leak & Storm Repair

Leaks don't wait. Neither do we.

A stain on the ceiling is the end of the story, not the start. We find where the water actually gets in, fix the roof, and repair everything it ruined on the way down.

Gulf Coast storms don't make appointments. When one comes through, you need somebody local who answers the phone, gets eyes on the damage fast, tarps what needs tarping, and gives you a straight price on the real fix.

Because we're a full repair contractor — not just a roofer — the same crew that stops the leak can replace the soaked sheetrock, repaint the ceiling, and fix the trim. One call, whole problem.

Storm and leak work

  • Leak tracing and repair — flashing, boots, valleys, penetrations
  • Storm damage response — tarping and emergency dry-in
  • Interior water-damage repair — sheetrock, ceilings, paint, trim
  • Chimney repair and re-flashing
  • Insurance-friendly written estimates with photos
