Home / Concrete & Flatwork

Concrete is forever. Pour it right once.

Formed straight, poured full, cured properly. Bad flatwork cracks in a year and there's no touching it up — which is why this is the last trade you bargain-shop.

Driveways, patios, walkways, and pads — the flatwork that frames everything else you do to your place. We handle grading and base prep, form it square, pour full depth, and finish it the way you pick: broom, smooth, or decorative.

We also tear out the old stuff. One of our BBB reviews is exactly that job: out with a worn deck, in with a clean concrete patio, good communication the whole way.

Flatwork we pour

  • Driveways — new pours, extensions, tear-out and replace
  • Patios — including deck-to-patio conversions
  • Walkways, steps and stoops
  • Slabs and pads — sheds, carports, equipment
  • Grading and base prep done before the truck ever arrives
