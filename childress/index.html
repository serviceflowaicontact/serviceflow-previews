Ground problems don’t fix themselves.

Baldwin County lots come with Baldwin County problems — low spots that hold water, thick brush and stumps, old structures in the way, driveways that wash out every storm. Here’s how we handle them.

Lot too rough to build on?

We clear the brush and trees, pull the stumps, and prep a solid pad — so your builder can start on schedule, not after another month of delays.

Water sitting after every rain?

Flat coastal ground needs help draining. Proper grading and resloping move the water away from your foundation instead of toward it.

Old structure in the way?

We demolish it, haul off the debris, and leave a clean site — one crew, one bill, no pile left behind for you to deal with.

What we do

One call covers the whole dirt phase of your project.

Site Preparation & Pads

House pads, barn pads, and building sites prepped, compacted, and ready for the slab crew.

Excavation

Digging, cutting, and moving dirt for foundations, ponds, utilities, and everything in between.

Land Clearing

Brush, trees, and stumps out — overgrown acreage turned into usable land.

Demolition

Old homes, barns, and structures taken down and hauled off, site left clean.

Driveways

New driveways installed and old ones prepped and re-based — built to survive Gulf Coast rain.

Grading & Resloping

Yards leveled and sloped the right way so water goes where it should.

Hauling & Fill Material

Topsoil, sand, gravel, clay, and fill dirt delivered — and debris hauled away.
