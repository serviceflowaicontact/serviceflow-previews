Sound familiar?

A stump doesn't just sit there. It gets in the way.

Whatever the tree left behind, the fix is the same — and it's faster and cheaper than most people expect.

Mowing around it every week

An old stump means trimming by hand, dodging it with the mower, and watching weeds and suckers sprout out of it every spring.

The fix: grind it below grade and mow straight over the spot.

Termites, ants & rot moving in

In South Alabama's climate, a dead stump becomes a buffet. Decaying wood invites termites and carpenter ants — and it's often just yards from your house.

The fix: remove the food source before it becomes a pest problem.

Blocking a project or a sale

A fence line, a new driveway, a shed pad, fresh sod before listing the house — one stump can stall the whole plan.

The fix: one visit, and the spot is clear and ready to build or plant.
